Three readers went through the same session independently, each in their own
`<report source="...">` block below: one read its sentiment, one found what
worked, one found what cost. Headings inside a block are that reader's own. You
are running the retrospective meeting. Turn their notes into one document a
person reads in two minutes and acts on tomorrow.

A retrospective is not a report card. The output is the change, and the columns
exist to earn it.

- **Merge.** The same event seen by two readers is one entry. Two readers
  reaching it is evidence; say so once and move on.
- **Cut what is not grounded.** An entry with no moment, no quote, and no
  consequence does not survive. Say how many you dropped; do not list them.
- **Resolve the conflicts.** The good-news column and the cost column often name
  the same act. Decide which reading the transcript supports, and say why.
- **Keep the person in it.** Findings about the person and about the agent both
  stay. A retrospective that only grades the agent is a self-review.

Emit exactly these four sections and nothing else:

    ## sentiment

Three lines at most: the shape of the session, the worst moment, the best
moment. Plain words.

    ## went well

The practices worth repeating, one line each, at most five. Each line ends with
the condition that should trigger it again.

    ## went wrong

What it cost and what caused it, one line each, worst first, at most five.
Mechanisms, never blame.

    ## next time

The whole point. At most four changes, ordered by what each one saves.

Each change is one line, and each line names three things: **who or what
changes** (the brief, the workflow, the person's first message, a check in the
loop), **the change itself**, and **the signal that will show it worked**. A line
that any session could carry is not a change; delete it. "Communicate better" is
not a change. "State the acceptance test in the first message, and the plan is
rejected without one" is a change.

End with one line: `Next time, first: <the single change with the largest
payoff>.` If the session gives you nothing to change, write `Next time: no
change.` instead, and do not pad the section to reach it.
