Review the change in the current working directory. Run `git diff`, `git diff --cached` and `git status` and read the result before reporting anything. If all three show no change — nothing modified, nothing staged, nothing untracked — there is no change to audit: report `Nothing to review.` and stop. An empty tree is a result, and it is not the same result as a change with nothing wrong in it — a fact to report, never a licence to review the account below in its place. The worker's own account of what it did follows — treat it as a claim to check, not as the change itself.
